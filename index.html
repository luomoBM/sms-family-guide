Santa Magic Sort for Families

A parent's guide to the free Christmas sorting game — what it is, what it teaches, and how sessions end. Verified 2026-11-17.

What it is

A browser sorting puzzle: tap a bottle, tap a second bottle, and one dose pours across. Win by making every golden centrepiece vessel hold one pure colour. No timers, no lives, no move limit, no download, no account — the rules and the game live on one page.

What it teaches

Planning in small units (doses), finishing what you start (centrepieces), and reading a system before acting — the site's own strategy guide is essentially a thinking curriculum.

How sessions end

The thirty-board ladder unlocks one card at a time, so any sitting has a natural stopping point. For variety nights, the collection adds a timed hex merge and an endless water sort.

School networks

The game lives on one official domain with no mirrors — the unblocked page explains what that means and recommends the honest route: a whitelist request.
